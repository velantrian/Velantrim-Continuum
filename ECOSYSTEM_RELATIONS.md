### Relationship to Continuum

Cognitive OS studies model replacement, behavioral qualities, anti-degradation and role evolution. Continuum independently tests what explicit state is sufficient for a process to continue across context/model/inference boundaries.

```text
Cognitive OS: did desired system/model behavior survive replacement?
Continuum:    can the process continue from explicit transferred state?
```

The projects can exchange research hypotheses and evaluation references, but Cognitive OS does not determine Continuum Gold/Oracle, Evidence Lock, architecture conclusions or live-write authority.

Current Continuum use of this relation is **research/shadow only** unless a later evidence-backed architecture decision explicitly changes that boundary.

`link ≠ dependency · related hypothesis ≠ evidence · pilot ≠ architecture authority`
